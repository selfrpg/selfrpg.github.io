Self.RPG

Self.RPG turns your habits into a character that grows. Plan your week, keep your quests, and earn XP for waking on time, focused work, meditation, training and more.

Contact

For help, questions or feedback, email self.rpg.support@gmail.com.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. If you reinstall Self.RPG or move to a new iPhone, tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen.

Your data

Everything Self.RPG records stays on your iPhone. You can export it at any time from Settings, Backup in the app, whether or not you are subscribed.
